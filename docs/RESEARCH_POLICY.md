# SUPERHERO Research Policy

## Core guardrails

- Identity ≠ role.
- Author ≠ originator.
- Witness ≠ perfect witness.
- Recorded ≠ invented.
- Translated ≠ authored.
- Later compiler ≠ contemporary source.
- Portrayed as hero ≠ objectively hero.
- Uncertainty is data.

## Human-first scope

v0.1 primarily models historical/deceased human actors: witnesses, participants, authors, chroniclers, scribes, translators, editors, researchers, reporters, and transmitters.

Mythological deities, spirits, monsters, and symbolic archetypes remain primarily MFTL entities.

## Living people

Do not use SUPERHERO as a people-profiling system. For living people, do not infer criminality, deception, secret motives, psychology, religious guilt, or other sensitive traits from weak or indirect evidence.

## Evidence rule

A relationship such as `witnessed`, `authored`, `recorded`, or `translated` must have source support. Earliest surviving recorder does not automatically mean inventor or originator.
