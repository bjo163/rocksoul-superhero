# SUPERHERO Roadmap

SUPERHERO is the canonical PERSON / actor / transmission layer for MoonWitness / Rocksoul Research. Product work is allowed only when it improves inspectability, provenance, reproducibility, accessibility, or research integrity. It must never turn PERSON data into hero ranking, moral scoring, psychology profiling, or vibes-based certainty.

## Guardrails for future work

Do not add a backend, mutable database, social graph, recommendation engine, hero/villain ranking, psychological score, or automated historical conclusion merely to expand the product.

A new feature is justified when it makes existing research more inspectable or safer to interpret. A new PERSON record is justified when it documents a real transmission chain.
